Sound when you clip
Plays on the PC the moment you press the hotkey. If the clip can't be saved, you hear two low notes.
Show "Clip saved" in the game
A small message in the top corner. It says "Saving clip..." the moment you press the hotkey, then "Clip saved" for 2 seconds. It never shows up in your clips. Works with borderless and windowed games. In exclusive fullscreen, Windows doesn't allow it, so you'll only hear the sound.
Tell me when files arrive on the PC
A sound and a small pop-up on the PC when your phone sends files.
Sort clips into game folders
Clips are named after the game and saved in a folder for it.
Clips folder
Where new clips are saved on the PC. ~ means your user folder.
Always count these as games
ClipDrop treats any program that fills the whole screen as a game. Add the program name of a game it misses, like one you play in a window. Separate names with commas.
Never count these as games
Fullscreen programs that aren't games, like video players. Browsers and Discord are already left out.
This device's name
Shown on files you send from this device, so you know where they came from. Only this device uses it.
Folder for shared files
On the PC. Files from your phone go in a "From (phone name)" folder inside it, and files the PC sends go in "From PC".
Send PC photos and videos to iPhone Photos
If you set up auto-send, photos and videos you send from the PC go straight into your iPhone's Photos app. Other files wait in the Files tab.
Your phone link
Open it in Safari on your phone (same Wi-Fi). Keep it private.
