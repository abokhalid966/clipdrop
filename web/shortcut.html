Auto-send to Photos

New clips (and photos or videos you send from the PC) go straight into your Photos app without opening ClipDrop.

Your link

You paste this while making the shortcut. Keep it private: anyone with it on your Wi-Fi can get your clips.

1. Make the shortcut

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + at the top right.
  2. Tap the name at the top and call it Get Clips.
  3. Tap Search Actions, type URL, and tap URL. Tap the blue text in it and paste your link.
  4. Search for Get Contents of URL and tap it.
  5. Search for Get Dictionary Value and tap it. Tap Key and type clips. It should read: Get Value for clips in Contents of URL.
  6. Search for Repeat with Each and tap it.
  7. Search for Get Contents of URL again and tap it. It goes inside the Repeat. It should read: Get contents of Repeat Item. If it doesn't, tap the blue part and choose Repeat Item.
  8. Search for Save to Photo Album and tap it. It also goes inside the Repeat, under the last step.
  9. Tap Done.

2. Try it

  1. Tap Get Clips once. Allow everything it asks: local network, connecting to your PC (Always Allow), and Photos. The first run only remembers the clips you already have, so nothing is saved yet. That's normal.
  2. Save a new clip on your PC (Ctrl+F9) and wait a few seconds.
  3. Tap Get Clips again. The clip should now be in Photos.

3. Make it automatic

When you open Photosbest
  1. In Shortcuts, tap Automation at the bottom, then + (or New Automation).
  2. Choose App, pick Photos, and tick Is Opened.
  3. Choose Run Immediately, tap Next, and pick Get Clips.
Open Photos after gaming and your new clips show up a few seconds later.
Tap the back of your iPhone
  1. Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → pick Get Clips.
When you get home
  1. New Automation → Wi-Fi → pick your home Wi-Fi → Run Immediately → Get Clips.
This only runs when your phone joins the Wi-Fi, not while it's already connected.
Home Screen button
  1. In Shortcuts, press and hold Get Clips → Share → Add to Home Screen.

If it doesn't work

  1. ClipDrop has to be running on your PC, and your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. If your PC's address changes (after a router restart), open this page again, copy the new link, and paste it into the URL step of the shortcut.
  3. Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → make sure Shortcuts is on.

Send to PC from the share menu

Adds Send to PC to the share menu in Photos, Files and other apps. It sends the original file in full quality. (Picking photos inside ClipDrop works too, but iPhone may shrink videos chosen that way.)

Your upload link

You paste this while making the shortcut. The end of the link (device=iPhone) is the name shown on your PC. You can change it.

1. Make the shortcut

  1. In Shortcuts tap + and name it Send to PC.
  2. Tap ⓘ at the bottom and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Tap Done. At the top, the shortcut now says "Receive Images and more from Share Sheet". That's right.
  3. Search for Repeat with Each and tap it. It should say: Repeat with each item in Shortcut Input.
  4. Search for Get Contents of URL and tap it. It goes inside the Repeat.
  5. Tap the blue URL in it and paste your upload link.
  6. Tap the arrow › to show more options: Method: POST · Request Body: File · File: Repeat Item
  7. Tap Done.

2. Use it

  1. In Photos, select one or more photos or videos and tap Share.
  2. Scroll down and tap Send to PC. The first time, allow it to connect to your PC (Always Allow).
  3. They appear in ClipDrop's Files tab marked From iPhone, and in Downloads\ClipDrop Shared on the PC.
Big videos take a moment. Keep the phone awake until it finishes.
